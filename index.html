About

I grew up in Oakland. I study international business and political science at George Washington University. In between, I started three things: a clothing resale operation that funded itself, TradeLead, and Aethelstan.

I build because it is the fastest way to find out whether an idea is any good. Technology is the tool I reach for first, business is the subject I am formally studying, and literature is what I read when I want to think in a different shape. They feed each other more than people expect.

Most of what I make now are systems that carry go-to-market work end to end without a person holding them upright. Not copilots that wait for a prompt, but agents that hold a brand, run on a schedule, publish, measure, and rewrite their own playbook from what the numbers actually said.

The part I care about is the boring half: getting a system to check its own work afterwards, and mark a run failed instead of writing it up as a success. That is what decides whether you can leave it running.

Before all this I argued competitively for years, and I still coach high school debate. It is the same job as the rest of it: work out what is actually true, then say it in a way that lands.

Ventures

Aethelstan

Founder & operator · 2026 – present · self-funded

aethelstan.media ↗

An operated agent for social media.

Podcasts and shows publish hundreds of episodes and almost none of it works as short-form. Aethelstan gives a show its own agent: it learns the brand, turns the long-form into a steady stream of clips, posts them on schedule, measures what happened, and uses that to make next week’s better. The owner directs and approves. They never open an editor.

Nothing about it is a shared model. Each customer’s agent holds their brand, their accounts and their goals, and its guidance is rebuilt from what has measurably worked on their accounts. Every run is verified against the calendar afterwards. A run that produced nothing is marked failed, not written up as a success.

  • 1personal agent per customer
  • 8specialists it runs
  • 48named tools, no free improvisation
  • 10+accounts operated
  • 200k+listeners behind them

Partnered with The Daily Beast. Sole founder and operator; scaled past $1k MRR without outside capital.

TradeLead

Founder · lead generation SaaS for tradespeople

Wound down

Leads for plumbers, electricians and roofers, delivered by text at 9:30 in the morning.

TradeLead found real local businesses, enriched them into named contacts, wrote the outreach for each one, and messaged the operator when the day’s batch was ready. Thousands of leads went out to local tradespeople.

The interesting constraint was cost. Tradespeople will not pay agency prices, so the whole stack had to run for less than a dinner: Supabase, Railway, Google Places, Hunter, Claude, Twilio, Stripe, roughly $15 a month at launch. Break-even was two customers. That is the only reason the price worked.

  • ~$15monthly infrastructure at launch
  • 2customers to break even
  • 1000sof leads delivered
